Privacy

This site is run by Open Numerics, LLC. It is a static website: there is no account system, no login and no database. What follows is the complete list of places your data can go.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which parts of this site people find useful. For example, whether visitors reach the services section, and which case studies get read.

Nothing is sent to Google unless you press "Accept" on the cookie banner. Until then the Google Analytics script is not even loaded. If you decline, no analytics request is made at all, and the site works exactly the same.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies to recognise repeat visits and records things like the pages you view, roughly how far you scroll, which buttons you press, your approximate location (city level, derived from your IP address), and your browser and device type. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising, and we do not track you across other websites.

Your choice is stored locally in your browser so you are not asked again. To change it, clear this site's data in your browser settings and reload.

Booking a call

Our booking pages are provided by Cal.com. When you open a booking window or schedule a call, your details, meaning your name, email address and anything you write in the booking form, are processed by Cal.com and added to our calendar. Cal.com's own privacy notice governs what they do with that data.

Email

If you email us, we keep the correspondence so we can reply and, where relevant, carry on a client relationship. Our mail is hosted by Google Workspace.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives standard server request data, including your IP address, as a technical necessity of serving pages.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, we do not run advertising or advertising pixels, we do not use cross-site trackers, and there are no third-party scripts on this site beyond the ones named above.

Your rights and how to reach us

If you are in the EU, EEA or UK you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected, and to have it deleted. Write to info@opennumerics.com and we will respond.
