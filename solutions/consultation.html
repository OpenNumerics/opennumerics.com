Improving the Performance of a Critical Piece of Code

s

A client in engineering design and advanced manufacturing relies on a critical piece of computational code running many high-accuracy interpolations. This code took nearly eight hours to complete, posing a debilitating bottleneck on our client's design workflow.

Our Contribution

We completed a performance analysis of the code, including profiling CPU and memory requirements, and identified the main bottleneck: a numerical kernel consisting of three nested loops. After an initial study, we were able to vectorize calculations and reduce to just one loop. Futher performance gains were possible by moving the numerical code to GPU for acceleration.

The Result for our Client

By surgically rewriting the old nested loops by a fully vectorized function, interpolations reduced from over eight hours to just two minutes in runtime. This removed the critical bottleneck for our client, enabling them to run up to 1,200x more interpolations and designs per day. Total project duration: 7 days.
