Technical expertise

The depth behind the results

Methods we've built, shipped and maintained in production — not a technology wish list.

Modeling & Simulation

PDE-based models and solvers at production scale — FEniCS, OpenFOAM and custom discretizations across deterministic, stochastic and multiscale systems.

Scientific Machine Learning

Surrogates, neural operators and hybrid physics-ML models that cut time-to-solution without giving up physical fidelity.

Automated Workflows

Systems that plan and run numerical work end to end — calling solvers, HPC jobs and data pipelines, with a human in the loop where it matters.

Custom Algorithms & Solvers

Fast linear and nonlinear solvers, adaptive time-stepping, particle methods and PDE-constrained optimization, built around the structure of your problem.

Uncertainty Quantification

Bayesian inference, stochastic sampling and rare-event analysis — predictions that arrive with calibrated confidence, not just point estimates.

GPU & HPC Acceleration

CUDA kernels, parallel solvers and Slurm-scale pipelines that move workloads from weeks to hours on hardware you already own.

Deployment & Integration

Onsite, private-cloud or containerized delivery, integrated with your existing systems and maintained after handover.
