# Open Numerics Website Revision v2
## Goal

This revision is **not** about making the website prettier.

It is about increasing the percentage of visitors that become qualified discovery calls.

Think like a conversion optimization exercise rather than a branding exercise.

---

# Core Positioning

Everything should reinforce a single idea:

> **We accelerate computational science and engineering.**

We solve computational bottlenecks.

Machine learning, scientific AI, HPC, GPUs, numerical methods and software engineering are the tools we use—not the products we sell.

Clients buy outcomes:

- Faster simulations
- Lower compute costs
- Faster R&D
- Automated scientific workflows
- Production-ready scientific software

---

# The Customer Journey

The homepage should naturally answer these questions in order:

1. Is this company for me?
2. Do they solve the kind of problem I have?
3. Can I trust them?
4. What is the easiest next step?

The website should tell a story rather than present a list of capabilities.

---

# Homepage Structure

## 1. Hero

The hero should immediately describe the customer's problem.

Visitors should understand within five seconds:

"If your simulations or computational workflows are too slow, too expensive or too difficult to scale, Open Numerics can help."

Possible headline:

**Accelerating Computational Discovery**

Supporting text:

We help scientific and engineering teams remove computational bottlenecks using numerical methods, machine learning, high-performance computing and modern software engineering.

Primary CTA:

**Book a 30-minute Computational Consultation**

Secondary CTA:

Explore Our Work

Immediately below:

- Faster simulations
- Lower HPC costs
- Scientific AI
- Production software

---

## 2. Industries We Serve

Before talking about technology, establish relevance.

Example:

Helping teams in

- Biotechnology
- Computational Chemistry
- Advanced Manufacturing
- Energy
- Materials Science
- Scientific Software
- Engineering Simulation

The visitor should immediately recognize themselves.

---

## 3. How We Help

This becomes the centerpiece of the homepage.

### Computational Performance Audit

Find computational bottlenecks before spending months optimizing.

Deliverables:

- bottleneck analysis
- performance roadmap
- expected ROI
- implementation recommendations

---

### Acceleration Sprint

Turn weeks of compute into hours.

Examples:

- GPU acceleration
- numerical optimization
- parallel algorithms
- workflow optimization
- scientific ML

Focus on measurable improvements.

---

### Scientific AI & Automation

Bring modern AI into scientific workflows.

Examples:

- surrogate models
- optimization pipelines
- AI-assisted simulation workflows
- intelligent workflow automation
- research copilots

Lead with customer value, not AI terminology.

---

### Build New Computational Products

From research prototype to production software.

Examples:

- simulation platforms
- scientific web applications
- APIs
- cloud-native scientific software
- digital twins
- engineering platforms

---

## 4. Shared Call To Action

Immediately after the four services:

### Not sure which service fits?

Every engagement starts with a free 30-minute computational consultation.

We'll discuss your computational workflow, identify potential bottlenecks and recommend the most valuable next step.

Button:

**Book a Consultation**

The four services should feel like possible outcomes of one conversation rather than four unrelated offerings.

---

## 5. Representative Results

Move existing case studies much higher.

Lead with measurable outcomes.

Examples:

- 17% → 44% reactor conversion
- 3× faster battery simulation
- AI agent optimizing PDE solvers
- Additional representative projects

Numbers build trust.

---

## 6. Technical Expertise

Keep the existing capabilities section.

Rename it to something like:

- Technical Expertise
- Core Technologies
- How We Work

This section supports credibility after visitors already understand the value proposition.

---

## 7. Insights / Projects

Keep technical articles, demonstrations and engineering projects.

These reinforce expertise.

---

## 8. Engagement Process

Reduce perceived risk.

Illustrate:

1. Book Consultation
2. Performance Audit
3. Acceleration Plan
4. Implementation & Measurement

This makes hiring Open Numerics feel straightforward.

---

# Conversion

The current website asks visitors to email.

This is too much friction.

Replace email as the primary conversion path with an online calendar booking (Calendly or equivalent).

Use "Book a 30-minute Computational Consultation" consistently across the site.

Keep email as a secondary option.

---

# Analytics

This redesign should be measurable.

Track at least:

- Hero CTA clicks
- Service CTA clicks
- Consultation bookings
- Scroll depth
- Case study clicks
- Contact interactions

The redesign should be evaluated using data rather than intuition.

---

# Writing Style

Avoid:

- generic AI buzzwords
- digital transformation language
- hype
- unnecessary jargon

Prefer:

- technically credible
- concise
- confident
- outcome-driven

Describe customer problems before describing technology.

Technology explains **how** we solve problems, not **what** we sell.

---

# Success Criteria

A first-time visitor should leave with four impressions:

1. These people understand my computational bottleneck.
2. They have solved similar difficult problems.
3. There is a clear, low-risk way to start working together.
4. I should book a consultation.
