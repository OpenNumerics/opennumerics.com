About Open Numerics

Open Numerics is a boutique computational science and engineering consultancy. We help you solve complex computational problems at the intersection of numerical simulation, machine learning, and high-performance computing. We work with clients in biotech, energy, advanced manufacturing, and scientific software to accelerate research, reduce computational costs, and build production-ready computational tools.

We intentionally remain small. Every engagement is led by our founder and staffed by senior technical experts, so your problem is never passed down through consulting layers.

Hannes Vandecasteele

Founder & Principal Computational Scientist

Hannes Vandecasteele founded Open Numerics after a career spanning computational science, academic research, and software engineering. He holds a PhD in Computational Chemistry from KU Leuven, completed postdoctoral research in Scientific Machine Learning and Applied Mathematics at Johns Hopkins University, and previously worked as a software engineer at Meta.

His work focuses on accelerating scientific discovery through numerical methods, machine learning, uncertainty quantification, and high-performance computing. He has developed new algorithms for molecular simulation, scientific machine learning, and large-scale computational modeling, with research published in leading journals and software deployed across research and industry.

At Open Numerics, Hannes works directly with you, from defining the computational strategy to designing and implementing production-quality solutions.
